# Clinical Trial Evidence Review

## Registry landscape

Define condition, intervention, population, study type, phase, geography/status and as-of date. Query broadly enough to estimate count, then deduplicate linked/duplicate records and retrieve only required fields.

For each record retain:

`stable_id | registry | status_date | study_type | phase | population | arms | primary_outcomes | enrollment | results_available | publication_link | caveat`

Verify current recruitment/status in the authoritative registry. Historical publications may not reflect the current record.

## Result review

1. Link registry, protocol/SAP where available, posted results and publication.
2. Compare prespecified and reported outcomes, timepoints, analysis populations and enrollment.
3. Extract arm-level denominators, effect estimate, uncertainty, missingness, discontinuation and harms.
4. Check randomization/blinding, multiplicity, early stopping, subgroup status and selective reporting.
5. Separate primary, secondary, exploratory and post hoc findings.
6. Interpret clinical magnitude as well as statistical uncertainty.

## Synthesis

- Do not pool heterogeneous trials unless population, intervention, comparator, endpoint and estimand are sufficiently aligned.
- Keep ongoing, completed-without-results, posted-results and published-results records distinct.
- Use trial design to judge what could be learned; use results to judge what was observed.
- Limit conclusions to studied population, intervention, comparator, endpoint and follow-up.

Stop and report when record linkage, outcome definitions or arm denominators cannot be reconciled.
