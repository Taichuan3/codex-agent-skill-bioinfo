# English Style Guardrails

Use for existing English scientific text after the section's job is clear.

## General

- Prefer precise, cautious prose over confident overstatement.
- Do not invent citations, mechanisms, datasets or novelty.
- Do not alter quantitative values unless the user explicitly asks to correct an error.
- Keep one main proposition per sentence where possible.
- Define abbreviations on first use.

## Overclaim words to soften

Flag:

- prove
- conclusively
- unprecedented
- best
- superior
- first
- demonstrate a mechanism

Safer options:

- show
- suggest
- support
- are consistent with
- to our knowledge
- in this dataset
- in this cohort

## Section stance

- Results: what was observed, under which conditions, with what support.
- Discussion: what it may mean, how it fits prior work, and where it may fail.
- Abstract: concise editorial signal, not a full data inventory.
- Methods: reproducible details, not rhetorical polish.
