# Scientific English writing profile

## Scope and priority

This is the shared reference for English polishing and Chinese-to-English
translation, not a fifth writing module. The path is retained for compatibility.
Fidelity and research integrity come first; explicit user/project requirements
and verified journal rules override this optional Nature-informed style.
Hashimoto is a separate author-requested module, not the default profile.
Do not restructure a spelling-only task or a literal translation.

## Argument before decoration

For substantial section revision, identify the section's question, the supported
answer, and the evidence that lets the reader accept it. Reverse-outline the
existing paragraphs: what does each add, and how does it change the argument?
Repair missing connections before changing individual words. This can be a
brief internal check, not a mandatory planning document or approval loop.

- Give each paragraph a discernible purpose; a question, necessary setup,
  observation and local inference can coexist when they advance one argument.
  Do not mechanically split them into four sentences or paragraphs.
- Put definitions before dependent claims. Explain the actual relation between
  statements rather than attaching a stock transition to every sentence.
- Keep the central comparison and its decisive evidence visible. Supporting
  checks can be shorter, but conflicting, negative or scope-changing results
  cannot be hidden to make a cleaner story.
- Preserve figure pointers, units, denominators, cohorts and uncertainty.
  A figure is an evidence anchor only when it was supplied and supports the text.
- Replacing repetitive prose is better than appending another explanation.
  Mature text may need no change; every edit should solve an identifiable problem.

## Results allocation and compression

Within the authorized scope, distinguish core findings, necessary support,
conclusion-changing qualifications and implementation detail. Main text should
carry the shortest chain sufficient to understand and assess the conclusion.
Detailed methods, diagnostics and redundant numeric inventories can be proposed
for Methods, captions or supplements. Preserve mandated statistics and complete
scientific information; relocation requires an existing or delivered destination.
If the task does not authorize that destination, suggest relocation in a note
and retain needed information rather than silently deleting it.

Never use selective reporting to conceal an unfavorable result. Keep effect
direction, study population, negative findings, sample units, uncertainty and
the statistical definitions that change interpretation. “Not significant” does
not establish equivalence. A model metric does not establish a mechanism.
Translation does not inherit permission to perform these allocation edits: keep
the Chinese source's information unless compression/restructuring was requested.

## Section-specific decisions

| Section | What the reader needs |
|---|---|
| Title | The concrete object and supported finding, without unsupported novelty |
| Abstract | The question, approach, principal answer and its meaningful boundary, within the requested length |
| Introduction | Enough context to locate the precise gap and understand why the study addresses it |
| Results | Observations, relevant comparisons, necessary QC and the uncertainty supporting each conclusion |
| Discussion | What findings jointly imply, how they relate to supplied prior work, alternatives and limits |
| Methods | Sample definitions, versions, parameters, exclusions and statistics sufficient for interpretation and replication |
| Existing caption | Original panels, n, units, tests, encoding and caveats; missing content belongs to figure-caption |
| Response letter | The actual concern, completed revision and remaining limitation; no invented completed experiment |

Discussion is not another Results summary. Make literature comparisons specific:
does the finding extend, qualify, contradict or support the cited work? Retain
only relationships supported by the supplied material. State which inference a
limitation constrains and, if appropriate, what test would distinguish plausible
explanations. Do not fabricate a citation or author commitment to future work.
Section functions guide choices; they are not fixed paragraph counts or required
openers/closers. Headings follow the user and target journal.

## Delivery and verification

Return the complete requested revision, not just an audit or outline. Verify
source-to-output numbers, direction, sample sets, terms, figure references,
negative findings, limitations and claim strength. Unknown facts remain unknown;
do not silently repair conflicting values. State whether source data were
actually checked when relevant. Editing quality is not scientific validation
or a guarantee of publication.

Keep local paths, workflow logs and internal labels outside reader-facing prose.
Retain unresolved scientific comments as separate author actions, not deleted
facts. Preserve provenance in the existing project system; a writing request
does not authorize analysis, directory moves or changes to source data.
